## What this document owes

- The Study object: question, optional hypothesis, what is held constant, what is varied, what evidence would support or weaken it, which Runs, which Readers, what was found, what remains uncertain (§20.2).
- The initial Study patterns as concrete protocols — Repeat Test, Small-Change Test, Try Many Settings, World Comparison, Plugin Comparison (§20.1).
- The inference stance: counts, proportions, distributions, paired comparisons, in ordinary language, with what was *not* tested stated alongside what was (§20.3, F-13).
- Study failure as a first-class recorded outcome distinct from every other failure class (§10).
- How a hypothesis is inferred from ordinary troubleshooting language and surfaced for human confirmation rather than imposed as ritual (§9, §20.2).
