## What this document owes

- What a Lab is permitted and required to define (§29's list), and the hard boundary that the core never learns domain meaning (§11).
- **The fit review as a procedure**, not a checklist in prose: how the nine §30 questions are run, who runs them, what evidence each requires, and what a pass, a conditional pass, and a failure each produce.
- Lab status vocabulary — candidate, experimental, validated — and the evidence threshold between them.
- The rule that a rejected Lab is useful evidence about SCR's boundary rather than an embarrassment to be hidden (§30, closing).
- Where each Lab's own paper lives (`../labs/`, Level 5) and what the core document may say about a Lab without importing its vocabulary.
