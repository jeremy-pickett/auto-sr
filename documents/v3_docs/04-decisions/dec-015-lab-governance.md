# DEC-15 — When is a Lab more than an experiment?

*Formal name: Lab governance. Cite this record as **DEC-15**.*

**Status:** open · **Who decides:** the project owner · **Kind:** boundary — the ladder exists; the height of its top rung is undecided

> **In one sentence:** every subject area (Lab) climbs a ladder — named, then reviewed and working, then backed by real accuracy evidence — and we have not yet set how much evidence that top rung requires.

---

## What this is about

Fields that answer to the public don't let tools promote themselves. A new medical test moves from research use toward clinical use in stages, each gated on evidence, and everyone can see which stage a test is at. The label *is* the safety mechanism.

Our ladder is already built, with three rungs:

- **Candidate** — named in the catalog. Sixty of these exist. Presence in the catalog proves nothing, and the catalog says so on its first page.
- **Experimental** — passed its fit review (ten hard questions: is local interaction even a defensible way to model this subject? where would it produce convincing nonsense? and so on). In use, accuracy not established.
- **Confirmed** — accuracy evidence exists against stated reference cases, to a stated extent.

Also settled: promotion is a human act, recorded with its evidence; a Lab can *fail* review, and a failure is kept as useful knowledge about where the platform stops working; and interfaces may never imply a status a Lab hasn't earned.

## What's open

The top rung's height: **what minimum evidence moves a Lab from experimental to confirmed?** One well-matched reference case? Several, across different regimes? Independent checking? Set it too low and "confirmed" becomes decoration; too high and nothing ever earns it and people learn to ignore the ladder entirely. Both failure modes end the same way — the label stops carrying information.

## What would make this easy to decide

> **Real data is coming for every Lab. Decide this against the first two or three Labs to reach the threshold, not in the abstract.**

The first candidates will teach us what evidence is actually obtainable, which is half of where the bar belongs.

## What this is blocking right now

- Nothing — no Lab is near the top rung yet. It becomes due when the first one is.
