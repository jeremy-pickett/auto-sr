# DEC-20 — How do we hand results to the real experts without overclaiming?

*Formal name: external calibration. Cite this record as **DEC-20**.*

**Status:** open · **Who decides:** the project owner · **Kind:** boundary — this is the platform's front door to the professions it hopes to serve

> **In one sentence:** when this platform finds a promising mechanism, the finding must eventually reach the people with the real tools — fire modellers, epidemiologists, security teams — and the package it arrives in must say exactly what it is: a lead, not a conclusion.

---

## What this is about

A tip line doesn't claim to have solved the case. It produces leads, hands them to investigators equipped to verify them, and the good ones know that a lead oversold is worse than no lead — it burns trust and wastes the receiving end's time.

That is this platform's exact position with respect to every field it touches. Our output is **candidate mechanisms**: simple rules that reproduce some behaviour of interest. A candidate that matches observations is a lead. It is not a finding about the real world, because — as the accuracy requirements spell out — many different mechanisms can reproduce the same observation, and telling which (if any) matches reality takes the field's own calibrated tools and data.

The rails are already law: nothing is described as verified or validated against the real world; claims are stated per regime, never per subject; every Lab states what further validation a promising mechanism needs before anyone treats it as a real hypothesis; and nothing is presented as fit for operational, safety, financial, or policy decisions. And the non-claims must **travel with the mechanism** — attached to the export itself, not left behind in documentation the recipient never saw.

## What's open

The package: what an export actually contains (the mechanism, its evidence, its confirmation status, its non-claims, in what format), how it remains verifiable after it leaves (the record-integrity machinery exists for this), and what the receiving expert can do with it without owning our platform.

## What would make this easy to decide

> **Pick the first real recipient — a fire-behaviour group, a security team, anyone — and design the package with them.** An export format designed without its recipient is a format designed twice.

## What this is blocking right now

- Nothing today; nothing has left the building yet.
- It gates the platform's first real-world handoff, which the per-Lab real-data phase will bring closer.
